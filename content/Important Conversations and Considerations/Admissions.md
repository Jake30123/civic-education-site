Who do we admit?
- Admitting people who already see the value of [[Civic Education]] is easy but not transformative and hard to assess. 

- Admitting people who do not see the value of [[Civic Education]] can be very transformative or destructive to the school culture if they do not eventually engage

- One possible solution is to admit students who do not necessarily see the value of [[Civic Education]] but are open minded to it

- We should make it clear in our [[Marketing]] that, if students have no will to learn about [[Civic Education]], this may not be the school for them

- While Olin has a lot to offer, they must be willing to accept all of Olin and be open minded to come here

How do we admit students to support culture?
