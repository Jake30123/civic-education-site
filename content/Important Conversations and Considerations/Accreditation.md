How do we integrate this with all of the other curricular plans, and fit it into a 4 year degree?
- The largest issue we kept running into was how to fit all of the required skills into a 4 year degree with credits
- There is a tradeoff between technical and non-technical content, Olin has consistently chosen to push the boundary of the non technical content

Since there is only so much time in 4 years, you can only add so much non-technical content without detracting from an engineers core understanding of their craft. But how far can that line of technical/non-technical be pushed? At what point are we no longer educating engineers?
- We believe that detracting from Olin’s technical education will be harmful for Olin’s future, and we should specifically focus on less effective parts of the curriculum
