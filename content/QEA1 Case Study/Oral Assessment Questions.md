# Post Module 1 & 2 Questions:

Question:

How does an identity matrix work? Why is an identity matrix important to learn? 

Purpose:

Try to get them to think about what each row and column really means.

  

Question:

Using a combination of visuals and words, visuals to explain shearing

Purpose:

Shearing is the weirdest of the matrices used in dancing animals so getting an understanding of how well they understand and can both visualize and talk about it is important. Try to push them to think about different ways of visualizing

  

Question:

Using a combination of visuals and words, explain the concepts of span and linear combination

Purpose:

Span and linear combinations are vital down the line for the eigenstuff and more. They are also an aspect of linear algebra that can be represented and understood really well as a visual. 

  

Question:

What is a determinate? Why does ad - bc = the determinate?

Purpose:

This stems off of what each row and column means and can get them thinking.

# Post Module 3 Questions:

Question:

Explain what it means to decompose a vector. What does each coefficient mean geometrically?

Purpose:

This helps test whether students understand decomposition as something more than just algebraic manipulation.

  

Question:

How can you know two vectors are orthogonal without using the word perpendicular?

Purpose:

Focuses more on the rhetorical skills and shows true understanding

  

Question:  
What are the benefits of distinguishing and using an orthonormal basis? Outside of just mathematics?

Purpose:

Help them try and find the connections and grounding within reality as that can both help with understanding and also their ability to explain.

  

Question:

Can you show visually and explain verbally what it means to project a vector onto a line or plane?

Purpose:  
Helps them look at more than just the mathematical steps taken. Generally just a good gauge for the module.

  

Question:

Why do we call it an orthogonal projection? What happens if it isn’t orthogonal?

Purpose:  
Helps them think of orthogonality and the whole process as not just mathematical jargon and leads to the intuition of shortest distance and orthogonality.
