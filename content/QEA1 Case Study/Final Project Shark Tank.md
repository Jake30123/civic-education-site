# Overview

You are working on a team of linear algebra product developers. Next month you are scheduled to give a TED talk on your newly released product. Your goal is to convince the sharks (Sam and David) that your product is worth investing in. 

# Project Objectives

Upon completion of this project, students should be able to:

  

- Demonstrate mastery of core linear algebra concepts
    
- Communicate this understanding clearly and concisely
    
- Apply linear algebra concepts to new applications that were not taught in class
    
- Think critically about the uses and limitations of linear algebra tools
    
- Consider the ethics of their product
    

# Deliverables 

  

1. The product
    

2. This product must use some linear algebra technique as its foundation (e.g. an image analysis software or a ML company)
    
3. The product must be of some usefulness to your target audience. Your target audience can be whoever you want. 
    
4. A product website/poster/informational video
    

5. The Pitch
    

6. Your team must give a 5 minute pitch to the sharks that details its purpose and strengths.
    
7. This pitch must also simply and concisely explain the mathematical concepts underlying your product. 
    
8. Describe a practical application of your product. 
    
9. Discuss the ethics and possible misuses of your product.
    

  

# Project Structure

1. You will be placed in a team of three. Each team member is expected to understand all components of the project from the pitch, math, to product. 
    
2. You will have three weeks to finish this product.
    

3. Week 1: Have an idea for the product and a rough MVP
    
4. Week 2: Have a final product idea done that has been mathematically verified along with a sketch for a pitch
    
5. Week 3: Practice and refine your pitch and deliver it to the the teaching team and other groups
