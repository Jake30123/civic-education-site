We are defining a civic education in the context of engineering education as equipping students with the competencies to develop a students [[Civic Identity]]. We will explore what these competencies are and what we mean by [[Civic Identity]]. This education must be a bedrock of the curriculum if it is to succeed, it will not simply emerge from a curriculum by sprinkling civic education competencies in classes in disconnected ways, in the same way that it would not work for engineering either.
