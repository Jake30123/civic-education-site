An ability to look at the world/a problem/a system/anything really, and have a nuanced opinion and understanding of it.
- No blissfully ignorant engineers.
- Critical awareness provides engineers the knowledge of when to exercise [[Critical Action]] action.

We made [[Discourse]] separate from [[Systems Understanding]] because it felt like a competency that could not be assessed or measured, and was supposed to be much more built out over time. [[Discourse]] allows you to take in other perspectives and other view points and process them, this builds your [[Systems Understanding]] which is your own knowledge of problems in a given system, and this culminates in your Critical Awareness of situations, problems, systems, whatever you want to say engineers work in.

Quotes from interviews:
“Critical awareness… is basically the idea to see and understand and explain what's going on in a system. A good or bad. It's tied… very much to systems thinking. And you can't develop it without experiences that are challenging and destabilizing and often depressing.” -Dr. Stolk
