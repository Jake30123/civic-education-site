Part of who we want an Olin Engineer to be: Some one who acts to alleviate suffering, and takes [[Critical Action]].

This is fundamentally an act of compassion.

Acts of compassion are competencies that need to be developed deliberately, just like [[Civic Education]] we cannot say it is simply emergent, it needs to be explicitly part of the curriculum.

Reflection is a big part of assessment of compassion.

In our current curriculum we do not provide a space to practice compassion.

It would also would defeat the intrinsic nature of compassionate acts if you could “fail compassion”, so applying [[Assessment Blob]] beyond identifying progress and narrative feedback may be redundant .
