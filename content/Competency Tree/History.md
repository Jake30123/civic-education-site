History of Technology is an example of a class that represents this, taught by Rob Martello, it encapsulates the need to give context to the work engineers are doing, and context can often mean history. A student must understand how tools and cultures they use and are a part of have hurt people in order to avoid it. We want to prevent repeats of history.

“Well-designed and well-taught history and sociology courses that teach the mechanisms of social disadvantage in a fine-grained way can give students the necessary foundation of knowledge for advancing the interests of socially disadvantaged groups. In addition to giving students relevant content knowledge, courses that educate students about the interests of marginalized groups might also dispose students to care more than they otherwise would about such groups.” (US Higher Education's Civic Responsibility to Educate for Informal Political Representation [Caitlin Murphy Brust](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/authored-by/Brust/Caitlin+Murphy), [Hannah Widmaier](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/authored-by/Widmaier/Hannah)).
