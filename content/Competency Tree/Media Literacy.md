Definition:
- Decode media messages (including the systems in which they exist)
- Assess the influence of those messages on thoughts, feelings, and behaviors
- Create media thoughtfully and conscientiously
Source: https://medialiteracynow.org/challenge/what-is-media-literacy/

“I think that college students of this age need media literacy and writing classes. … Without those things, the student body, without prior knowledge, or without like taking demand, we just don’t have people who can read media critically. And that is becoming increasingly important outside of the context of Olin, but like in the world. If you are not media literate, you are taken advantage of in the culture.” -Maddy Fahey (In an interview with Frankly Speaking)

Related Skills:
- Understanding how information is conveyed (Propaganda, etc.)
- Understanding media's critical role in governments and society
- How to find the information to form opinions/make decisions [[Discourse]]
- Allows the intake of media to better inform a students [[Systems Understanding]]
