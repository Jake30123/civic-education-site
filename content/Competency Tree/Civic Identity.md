 This idea is a little more broad, but it is essentially a person's personal morals and values as applied to societal and political issues. The name of this concept may change depending on people's opinions of it, but that is the idea. We want Oliners to have an education that builds competencies and provides opportunities that allow a student to grow or act on their own civic identity.

Is a core part of a students [[Professional Identity]], and is built off of and continues to influence a students [[Critical Awareness]] and [[Critical Action]] and [[Discourse]].

Dr. Jon Stolk talked a lot about how critical awareness and critical action come together to create a comprehensive [[Civic Education]].

Dr. Erhardt Graeff also talked a lot about civic identity and its ties to professional identity.

Quotes:
- “We take seriously personal and professional identity development as it's tied to civic duty... it’s actually defined in engineers’ responsibility to society and make that part of how we think about what your professional identity will be.” - Dr. Graeff

- “This thing that I would refer to as critical awareness that gets coupled to critical action to make up something that you might call a more holistic kind of way of acting consciously or responsibly in the world.” - Dr. Stolk
