Discourse is incredibly important to understanding other perspectives as well as your own, and having the ability to facilitate positive discourse can transform how you approach and resolve conflict and disagreement.

Rhetoric is the ability to formulate an opinion/argument eloquently and effectively. Essentially the ability to convince. Members of Frankly Speaking and Dr. Graeff spoke to us about Rhetoric and its impact:

“We get a lot of rants… They’re very train of thought, kind of hard to follow… It’s just like, I’m mad about this thing and I’m going to tell everyone about it… I wish people would be more actionable in a way that’s… analyze the situation a little bit more… and frame it less as like… I’m mad about this.” -Frankly Speaking

“Even if it’s not like some super in-depth thing… it’s like remembering that your audience is everyone and that they should be taking something away from whatever you’re writing.” -Frankly Speaking
