Communities of Practice:

This blob is not very fleshed out, so there is not a lot of info here. But the idea is any group people working together to achieve a goal. For us this could mean labs or project teams, experimental or traditional classes, or even clubs and identity groups. How we want to structure "classes" in a new curriculum is currently fluid, so we do not yet know how our version of Civic Education fits into COPs. It is potentially a great place for the curriculum to provide students with contexts to practice and develop their [[Critical Action]].
